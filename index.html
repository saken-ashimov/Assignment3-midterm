A reboot that stayed with Caesar

In 2011 20th Century Fox restarted the Planet of the Apes series. Instead of copying the 1968 film, the new trilogy told how the apes rose, step by step, and put one character at the centre: Caesar, played through motion capture by Andy Serkis.

The effects were made by Weta Digital. The apes are not men in masks: every look, every sign-language gesture and every pause comes from a real actor's performance.

Each film moves the conflict up a level: first an escape, then a fragile peace, then open war.

Six faces of the war

The characters you need to know to follow the story.

Caesar

Andy Serkis

Raised by humans, leader of the apes. He wants peace and pays for it every time.

Koba

Toby Kebbell

A bonobo scarred by lab experiments. He cannot trust any human.

Maurice

Karin Konoval

A calm orangutan from a circus who speaks in sign language and stays loyal to Caesar.

Rocket

Terry Notary

A chimpanzee who starts as Caesar's rival and becomes one of his closest allies.

Bad Ape

Steve Zahn

A zoo chimpanzee who survived alone and brings humour into the darkest film.

The Colonel

Woody Harrelson

The human commander in War for the Planet of the Apes, Caesar's opposite number.

The three films

Rise of the Planet of the Apes

2011 · Director: Rupert Wyatt

A scientist tests a drug on chimpanzees. His adopted ape Caesar grows smarter than anyone expected and finally leads an escape.

Dawn of the Planet of the Apes

2014 · Director: Matt Reeves

Ten years later a virus has wiped out most people. Apes and the last humans try to share the forest, but fear on both sides breaks the peace.

War for the Planet of the Apes

2017 · Director: Matt Reeves

Caesar hunts the Colonel who destroyed his family, and the trilogy ends with a question about what a leader owes to his people.
